Privacy Policy — Crypto Accountant

Last updated: October 15, 2026 · Effective immediately
Applies to the Android application “Crypto Accountant” («Crypto Accountant», package com.usdtincome.app).

Summary / خلاصه

EN: Crypto Accountant is an on-device bookkeeping app. There is no account and no developer-operated cloud. Your data (wallet addresses, manual entries, settings) is stored only on your device. The only data sent off the device are the public wallet addresses you choose to enter, which are queried against public blockchain and price APIs to show balances, transactions and exchange rates. We never ask for seed phrases or private keys. We do not collect analytics, advertising IDs, or personal information, and we do not sell data.

FA: «Crypto Accountant» یک برنامهٔ حسابداری روی دستگاه است. حساب کاربری و سرور ابری متعلق به توسعهدهنده وجود ندارد. اطلاعات شما (آدرس کیف پولها، ثبتهای دستی و تنظیمات) فقط روی دستگاه شما ذخیره میشود. تنها دادهای که از دستگاه خارج میشود، آدرسهای عمومی کیف پولی است که خودتان وارد میکنید و برای نمایش موجودی، تراکنشها و نرخ ارز به APIهای عمومی بلاکچین و قیمت فرستاده میشود. ما هرگز سید فریز یا کلید خصوصی نمیخواهیم. هیچ دادهٔ تحلیلی، شناسهٔ تبلیغاتی یا اطلاعات شخصی جمعآوری نمیشود و دادهها فروخته نمیشوند.

1. Data we store (on your device only)

  • Public wallet addresses you enter (EVM, Bitcoin, XRP, TON, TRON).
  • Manual income/expense entries, investments list, and app settings (currency, spend percentage, minimum cash, API keys you optionally provide).
  • Support-section state (whether the optional support prompt was dismissed) and sync counters.

This data is kept in the app’s local storage on your phone. It is not transmitted to us. You can delete everything at any time from Settings → Clear all data, or by uninstalling the app.

2. Data sent to third-party services

To display balances, transactions and prices, the app contacts the following public services. Only the public addresses you entered (and technical request data such as IP address, which any internet request includes) are transmitted:

  • Public blockchain nodes / RPCs — bsc-dataseed (Binance), publicnode.com endpoints, Blockstream (blockstream.info) for Bitcoin, Ripple public servers (s1.ripple.com, xrplcluster.com), toncenter.com for TON, api.trongrid.io for TRON — to read balances.
  • Transaction indexers — Etherscan API v2 (api.etherscan.io), NodeReal (bsc-mainnet.nodereal.io), Blockscout instances (eth.blockscout.com, polygon.blockscout.com, arbitrum.blockscout.com, base.blockscout.com, optimism.blockscout.com) — to read transaction history.
  • Price APIs — CoinGecko (api.coingecko.com) for crypto prices; open.er-api.com for fiat exchange rates.

These requests are necessary for the app’s core functionality (fetching your own on-chain data). We do not receive, store, or process this data on our own servers. Each provider processes requests under its own privacy policy.

3. Voluntary support (no payments in the app)

The app is free — it has no in-app purchases, no subscriptions and no Google Play Billing. It contains an optional “Support the project” section that simply displays the developer’s public crypto wallet addresses (for example USDT, BTC). Nothing is sent to the developer automatically: an address leaves your device only when you choose to copy it or show its QR code, and any transfer happens entirely in your own wallet app. The app cannot access your wallet and never asks for keys. Sending funds is voluntary and non-refundable; always double-check the address and network.

4. What we do NOT collect

  • No account, name, email, phone number or contacts.
  • No analytics SDKs, ad SDKs, crash-reporting SDKs.
  • No seed phrases or private keys (the app never asks for them).
  • No location data, no photos, no files beyond the backup file you explicitly export/import.

5. Permissions

  • Internet — to fetch blockchain, price and exchange-rate data.
  • Notifications (POST_NOTIFICATIONS) — only if you enable monthly summary reminders; can be disabled any time in system settings.
  • Vibration (VIBRATE) — for optional haptic feedback on taps; can be disabled in the app (Settings → Display & feel).

6. Security & financial safety

The app only ever asks for PUBLIC wallet addresses. It cannot move, spend or access funds. Never share seed phrases or private keys with anyone, including apps claiming to need them. Local API keys you optionally enter (Etherscan/NodeReal) are stored on your device and sent only to those services.

7. Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from children.
